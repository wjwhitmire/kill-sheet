      Method and limits

      Standard surface-stack kill sheet formulas as taught in IADC WellSharp and IWCF well-control courses. Oilfield units throughout; metric input is converted first and results are converted back for display. Constant 0.052 psi/ft per ppg.

      KMW = OMW + SIDPP / (0.052 x TVD) rounded up to the next 0.1 ppg (10 kg/m3 in metric) ICP = SCR pressure + SIDPP FCP = SCR pressure x KMW / OMW

      Volumes. Each section is length x capacity. Surface to bit adds the surface line volume. Bit to shoe is the annular volume over the open hole length (well MD minus shoe MD). Strokes = volume / pump output. Minutes = strokes / kill rate.

      Wait and Weight schedule, vertical. Straight line from ICP at the start (after surface lines) to FCP at surface-to-bit strokes, in 10 equal stroke steps.

      P(step i) = ICP - (ICP - FCP) x i / 10

      Wait and Weight schedule, deviated. At the bottom of each string section (measured depth md, true vertical depth tvd), with KMW filling the string down to that point:

      P = ICP - SIDPP x (tvd / TVD) + (FCP - SCR) x (md / MD)

      The first term removes static overbalance as the KMW column grows in vertical height. The second adds the extra friction of heavier mud in proportion to the length filled. The 10-step table is read off straight lines between these points. When every section TVD equals its MD this reduces exactly to the vertical straight line. The section points are also shown for vertical wells; in a tapered string they sit off the straight line (a well-known effect), and the table reports the difference.

      Driller's method. First circulation: original mud, hold drill pipe pressure at ICP at the kill rate until the influx is out (at least one full circulation). Shut in; SICP should equal SIDPP. Second circulation: pump KMW, hold casing pressure constant at the shut-in value until KMW reaches the bit, then hold drill pipe pressure at FCP until KMW is at surface.

      MAASP = (LOT EMW - MW) x 0.052 x shoe TVD LOT EMW from a test = test MW + leak-off surface pressure / (0.052 x shoe TVD)

      Influx. Height comes from filling the annulus from the bit upward with the pit gain, spilling into the next section when one fills. Gradient and type:

      influx gradient = 0.052 x OMW - (SICP - SIDPP) / height gas below 0.25 psi/ft; oil, condensate or mixed 0.25 to 0.40; water above 0.40

      Barite at SG 4.2 (35.0 ppg), 100 lb sacks:

      barite lb/bbl = 1470 x (KMW - OMW) / (35 - KMW) volume increase per bbl = (KMW - OMW) / (35 - KMW)

      Limits.

      • Surface BOP stack only. No subsea choke line friction, no riser margin.
      • Single-bubble, static view. Gas expansion, migration and temperature are not modeled. MAASP is a static figure at the shoe and changes as soon as KMW passes the shoe.
      • Influx type is indicative only. It is distorted by inaccurate pit gain, washed-out hole, mixed influx, a float in the string, influx in the string, and inclination near bottom (height here is along hole).
      • FCP from KMW / OMW assumes friction scales with mud weight only. Rheology changes, and SCR taken at a different depth or mud, are not corrected.
      • The bit is assumed on bottom: string and annular lengths must each add to the well MD within 1 ft.
      • Barite figures assume clean weighting of the active volume only. No dilution, no trip margin, no safety margin is added to KMW.
      • Inputs outside 5 to 25 ppg, negative pressures, zero pump output, shoe below TD, and section lengths that do not close are refused. Unusual but possible readings (such as SICP below SIDPP) are flagged, not refused.
